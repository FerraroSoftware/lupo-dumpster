## Understanding Estate Cleanouts

**Definition of an Estate Cleanout:** An estate cleanout involves clearing all the contents from a property and sorting through items to determine what will be kept, donated, sold, or disposed of. It's a comprehensive process that can be emotionally taxing and physically demanding.

**Circumstances Necessitating an Estate Cleanout:** Estate cleanouts are typically required after a loved one has passed away. Still, they can also be necessary for downsizing, relocating, or when the accumulation of possessions becomes unmanageable. Understanding the triggers can help mentally and logistically prepare for the process.

**Key Components of Estate Cleanouts:** The process involves several key steps – sorting through personal belongings, determining the value of items, disposing of waste, and possibly preparing a home for sale. Each step can be intricate, requiring careful consideration and sensitivity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I rent a dumpster or hire a cleanout crew?

Rent a dumpster if family members can do the loading. It's the lowest cost per load. Hire a crew if you live out of town, you're short on time, or the home is full of heavy furniture.

### What size dumpster do I need for an estate cleanout?

A [20-yard dumpster](/services/20-yard-dump-trailer), which holds about 6–7 pickup-truck loads, fits most whole-house cleanouts. A smaller home or a single floor may only need a 15-yard.

### How do we start an estate cleanout?

Walk the home first and mark what the family is keeping, selling, and donating. Everything left is what goes in the dumpster or out with the crew.
