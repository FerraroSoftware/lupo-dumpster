Every fall, Pasco County gets a little busier. Snowbirds head back to New Port Richey, Holiday, Hudson, and Port Richey, and seasonal rentals start filling up. If your Florida home sat closed up all summer, it probably needs more than a quick dusting.

Florida summers are hard on stored things. Here's how to get the house, the lanai, and the garage ready before the season starts.

## Why a Snowbird Home Cleanout Matters

A home that's been closed up through a Florida summer has dealt with months of heat, humidity, and afternoon storms. Even with the AC set to run, garages, sheds, and lanais don't get that protection.

At the same time, summer is when a lot of things get "temporarily" stored. Old patio chairs get pushed into the garage. Boxes from a family visit end up in the spare room. Storm debris gets piled by the side of the house.

Clearing it out before the season does a few things:

- **You arrive to a home that's ready**, not a project.
- **Renters get a clean, open space**, which matters if you rent seasonally.
- **You catch moisture problems early**, before they spread.
- **You free up the garage and lanai** for the months you'll actually use them.

## Your Snowbird Home Cleanout Checklist

Work through the home one area at a time:

| Area | What to clear or check |
| --- | --- |
| Lanai and pool area | Faded or broken patio furniture, cracked planters, mildewed cushions, old grills |
| Garage | Boxes stored over summer, old paint cans, broken tools, anything with a musty smell |
| Shed | Rusted equipment, rotted wood, pest-damaged items |
| Spare rooms and closets | Clothing and linens with mildew spots, swollen furniture, old electronics |
| Kitchen and pantry | Expired food, old small appliances |
| Yard | Palm fronds, fallen oak branches, storm debris piled along the fence |
| Bedrooms | Mattresses that smell musty or show stains |

### Start With the Lanai

The lanai is usually the first thing you see and the place you'll spend the most time. It also takes the most abuse from sun and rain.

Clear out:

- Patio furniture with cracked straps, rusted frames, or brittle plastic
- Cushions with mildew spots or a musty smell
- Broken umbrellas, dead plants, and cracked pots
- Old grills you're replacing

Propane tanks can't go in a dumpster. Take them to an exchange or a retailer that accepts them.

Once it's cleared, rinse the screens and floor, and the lanai is ready for morning coffee again. Clearing the lanai before a storm also keeps loose items from turning into projectiles, and hurricane season runs through November 30.

### Tackle the Garage

The garage is where summer clutter goes to hide. It also gets hot and humid, which is tough on boxes, paper, and fabric.

Go shelf by shelf. Open every box you can. If it smells musty, has water stains, or shows signs of pests, it's usually time to let it go.

If the garage is more than a weekend job, our [garage cleanout service](/services/garage-cleaning-service) can handle the heavy lifting.

### Check for Humidity Damage Inside

Even with the AC running, Florida humidity finds its way in. Look for:

- **Mildew** on clothing, linens, shoes, and leather
- **Swollen or peeling particleboard** furniture
- **Musty mattresses**, especially in spare rooms that stayed closed
- **Warped paper and photos** in closets and drawers

Damaged mattresses are a common find. Our [mattress disposal](/services/mattress-disposal) service hauls them away so you don't have to wrestle them into a car.

### Haul Off Old Furniture

## Getting It Done Before Arrival

Timing is the hardest part, especially if you're still up north. A few tips:

1. **Book a few weeks ahead.** Fall is busy around Pasco County as everyone gets ready for the season.
2. **Line up someone local.** A neighbor, property manager, or family member can let a crew in or point out what goes.
3. **Send photos.** If you're coordinating from out of state, photos of each area make planning easier.
4. **Plan the cleanout before any cleaning service.** Haul first, then clean, so you're not cleaning around piles.
5. **Leave time for repairs.** If you find water damage, you'll want a few weeks to get it fixed.

A few weeks before you or your guests arrive is ideal. That leaves time to sort, haul, and clean without a last-minute rush. Hurricane season runs through November 30, so clearing the lanai and yard early also means fewer loose items if a late storm comes through.

### What humidity damage should I look for?

Musty or mildewed cushions and mattresses, swollen particleboard furniture, rusted patio furniture, warped boxes, and anything stored in a closed-up garage or shed over the summer.

### Can old propane tanks or paint go in the dumpster?

No. Propane tanks, batteries, wet paint, and hazardous chemicals aren't allowed. Take them to an exchange, retailer, or your county's household hazardous waste drop-off.
