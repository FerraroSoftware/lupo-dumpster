# StoryBrand 2.0 BrandScript — Lupo Dumpster Rentals

> **Read this before writing any headline, section, ad, email, or GBP post.** This is the messaging source of truth for the rebuild. Every page is built from these pieces. If copy doesn't map to one of the elements below, it probably doesn't belong on the page.
>
> **Status: DRAFT v1 (Oct 2026).** Built from the current site, reviews, and `README.md`. Items marked **(confirm)** need Alex's sign-off. Terminology follows *Building a StoryBrand 2.0*. If the book's worksheet wording differs from what's here, match the book and update this doc.

## 0. The core rule

**The customer is the hero. Lupo is the guide.**

The current site talks about Lupo ("#1 Dumpster Rental Service," "How We Compare," "About the Founder" near the top). The rebuild flips it so every section answers one of three questions a visitor is asking within 5 seconds:

1. **What do you offer?** A flat-rate dumpster dropped in your driveway, often same day.
2. **How will it make my life better?** The mess is gone, the project gets done, no surprise bill.
3. **What do I need to do to get it?** Call, text, or book online.

If the hero section of any page doesn't answer all three, it fails.

## 2. The Problem

StoryBrand 2.0 layers problems from surface to soul. Customers buy to solve the **external** problem, but they choose a company because it resolves the **internal** one.

| Layer | Lupo version |
| --- | --- |
| **Villain** (the root cause, personified) | **Hidden fees and no-show haulers.** The national broker that quotes one price and bills another; the guy who says "Tuesday" and shows up Friday. |
| **External problem** | A pile of junk, debris, or roofing tear-off and no easy way to get rid of it. |
| **Internal problem** (how it makes them feel) | Overwhelmed, stuck, and worried about getting ripped off or stood up. The project stalls. The garage stays full. |
| **Philosophical problem** (why it's just plain wrong) | **Cleaning up your own property shouldn't come with surprise fees and broken promises.** |

**Copy usage:**
- Hero subhead hints at the external problem.
- The "stakes" section names the internal problem.
- The philosophical line is the brand's rallying cry. Use it in About, ads, and the one-liner.

## 3. The Guide (Lupo)

A guide needs **empathy** (we get it) and **authority** (we can fix it). Show both, briefly. The guide is never the hero.

### Empathy statements (pick one per page)

- "We know a stalled project is stressful. You just want the mess gone."
- "Nobody wants a surprise bill for their own cleanup."
- "We're your neighbors in Holiday. We answer our own phone." *(confirm: does Alex/Amanda answer directly?)*

### Authority (proof). Use real, verifiable items only

| Proof | Value | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Review count | **160+** *(site also says 130+, 144; reconcile)* | ⚠️ (confirm) |

## 4. The Plan

### Process plan (3 steps, every page)

1. **Pick your size & date.** Call, text, or book online. Get your flat price in under a minute.
2. **We drop it off.** Right where you need it, often same day.
3. **Fill it, and we haul it away.** Call when you're done, and it's gone.

*(Name it something ownable, e.g. "The Lupo 1-2-3." Optional.)*

### Agreement plan (promises that kill the fear)

- **Flat-rate pricing.** The price we quote is the price you pay. Delivery, pickup, and disposal up to the weight limit included.
- **No hidden fees.** Any possible extra (overweight, extra days, prohibited items) is listed up front.
- **On-time delivery.** *(confirm: any guarantee we'll stand behind?)*
- **Driveway-friendly.** *(confirm: board/plywood protection or trailer tires — what do we actually do?)*
- **Local, owner-operated.** You talk to Lupo, not a call center.

## 6. Stakes: Failure (what happens if they don't act)

Use sparingly (a pinch of salt, not the whole meal). One short section per page.

- Weekends lost to dump runs in the pickup truck
- Surprise overage and "fuel" fees from a national broker
- A roofing crew standing around waiting for a dumpster that didn't show
- A project that drags on for weeks
- HOA or code-enforcement notices for debris piles *(confirm local relevance)*
- A pulled back hauling a couch you shouldn't have lifted

## 7. Success (what life looks like after)

- A clear driveway and a garage you can park in again
- The project finished on schedule
- One flat price, no surprises
- A listing or rental turned over on time
- Your weekend back

## 9. The One-Liner

The one-liner is the most-reused asset: GBP description opener, About page, email signatures, truck magnet, Facebook bio, how Alex answers "what do you do?"

**Formula:** Problem → Solution → Result

> **Draft A (recommended):** Most Pasco County homeowners and contractors lose whole weekends to dump runs and get hit with surprise dumpster fees. Lupo drops a flat-rate dumpster in your driveway, often the same day, and hauls it away when you're done, so you get your space and your weekend back.

> **Draft B (shorter):** Cleanup projects stall when hauling is a hassle and prices keep changing. Lupo delivers flat-rate dumpsters across Pasco and Pinellas, often same day, so you can finish the job and get your space back.

**Tagline candidates** (pick one, use everywhere):
- "Flat-Rate Dumpsters. Delivered Fast."
- "Your Mess. Our Muscle. One Flat Price."
- "Local Dumpsters. Honest Prices."

## 12. Email / SMS Sequences (StoryBrand nurture + sales)

Triggered by the lead generator or an unbooked quote. Requires SMS consent (see `conversion.md`).

**Nurture (after lead magnet, weekly-ish):**
1. Deliver the guide + "here's your recommended size" + book link
2. "What can't go in a dumpster in Florida" (objection removal)
3. Real customer story (before/after photos)
4. "Do I need a permit?" (Pasco/Pinellas)
5. Seasonal / reminder + review proof

**Sales (after unbooked quote, short):**
1. Same day: "Your flat-rate price for a {size}: ${price}. Book here" (SMS + email)
2. Day 1: Proof (reviews) + "we can usually deliver tomorrow"
3. Day 3: Objection (weight/driveway/what's included)
4. Day 5: Last call / availability nudge
